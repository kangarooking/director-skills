# Module: Brand Promo

## Objective
Build recognition, emotional response, and a memorable brand association. The film should feel like one designed idea rather than unrelated beautiful shots.

## Required inputs
- brand positioning and audience;
- approved logo, color, typography, product and visual assets;
- core message and proof points;
- desired emotion and desired audience response;
- mandatory copy, exclusions, and platform specifications.

## Workflow
1. Extract the brand's promise, personality, audience tension, and proof.
2. Create a central visual or motion mnemonic: a recurring shape, material, gesture, sound, or transformation.
3. Design an emotional arc: establish → develop → intensify → resolve.
4. Build a visual bible and identify which brand assets must be exact.
5. Select a motion language that reinforces the brand's meaning.
6. Pilot the hero shot and the ending/brand reveal early.
7. Check that the logo and final claim are accurate, readable, and given sufficient screen time.

## Brand-specific QA
- Is the brand recognizable without relying only on a logo at the end?
- Is the visual system consistent?
- Does the emotional arc build toward a clear payoff?
- Are brand colors, product appearance, and mandatory copy correct?
- Are claims supported and approved?
- Does the final shot resolve the film rather than simply stop?

## Prompt guidance
Use the brand guide as an authority for identity and the style reference as an authority for lighting or mood. Do not ask the model to invent or redraw a precise logo. Composite approved logo and copy in post-production.
