# Example Blueprint — Science Explainer

**Status:** Workflow example only; no generated clip and no scientific claim is being asserted.

## Hypothetical brief
Explain a simple heat-transfer concept to a general audience in 30 seconds.

## Proposed narrative
- Hook: Why does one material feel colder than another?
- Establish: Show the same environment and contact condition.
- Mechanism: Visualize energy transfer schematically.
- Clarify: The visual is a simplified model, not a literal microscopic camera view.
- Close: Summarize the causal principle.

## QA focus
Verify the mechanism against a reliable source; label the schematic if needed; avoid visual metaphors that imply a false direction or scale.
