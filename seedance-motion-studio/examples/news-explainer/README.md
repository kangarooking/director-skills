# Example Blueprint — News Explainer

**Status:** Workflow example only; not a report about a real event and no footage has been generated.

## Hypothetical brief
A 45-second explainer about a city announcing a new transit plan. All dates, route maps and budget figures must come from cited official sources.

## Proposed narrative
- Hook: What changed?
- Timeline: Announcement and next milestones.
- Evidence: Official map/data shown with source labels.
- Impact: Clearly separate confirmed impact from analysis.
- Close: What remains unknown?

## Visual strategy
Use authentic documents/maps and editorial graphics for evidence. Use AI-generated city imagery only as clearly illustrative atmosphere, not as real footage of the announcement or construction.
