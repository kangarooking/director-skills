# Example Blueprint — Brand Promo

**Status:** Workflow example only; no brand or clip has been selected or generated.

## Hypothetical brief
A 15-second brand film for a design-focused consumer product.

## Proposed narrative
- 0–3s: Introduce a distinctive material/shape.
- 3–8s: Repeat the brand's motion mnemonic in two contexts.
- 8–12s: Build to the hero reveal.
- 12–15s: Resolve to approved logo and claim in post.

## QA focus
A coherent visual mnemonic, consistent color/material, approved claims, exact logo, and an ending with enough hold time to register.
