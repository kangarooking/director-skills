# Example Blueprint — Product Launch

**Status:** Example of workflow artifacts only; no Seedance clip has been generated.

## Hypothetical brief
A 20-second launch film for a small desktop productivity device. The real product images and approved logo are supplied by the user.

## Proposed narrative
- 0–3s: Product hero reveal.
- 3–7s: Macro detail of a key physical feature.
- 7–12s: One authentic product interaction.
- 12–16s: Show the verified outcome.
- 16–20s: Product hero and approved logo/CTA added in post.

## Key risks
- Product geometry drift;
- generated UI/text becoming inaccurate;
- feature behavior being invented;
- logo distortion.

## Production choice
Use Seedance for controlled product atmosphere and motion where suitable; use real product footage/screens and deterministic post-production for exact features and text.
